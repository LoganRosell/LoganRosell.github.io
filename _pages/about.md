I am a economics and policy nerd currently completing a Master’s of Data Science at Willamette University. My background in health-care IT, revenue operations, financial analysis, and academic research informs my analytical and curious mindset. 

I am driven by a desire to understand the world not as it appears but as it works. My interests span many disciplines empowering me to attack old problems from new angles and hopefully to make lasting contributions to the teams and organizations I am a part of. In addition to growing my own understanding, I find deep satisfaction in sharing what I’ve learned with others through teaching and leadership opportunities which I have been lucky enough to engage as a tutor in college and as a mentor and lead in my professional career.  

My focus on building data-driven processes to resolve the source rather than the symptom of an issue contributed to one of my recent success at Epic. Facing one of the largest error back-logs in the company, I expanded the team and divided it into subgroups which became responsible for specific KPIs linked to our application’s pool of unresolved errors. Within 30 days of starting this new system the team managed to halve our backlog. 

I am currently working to find my next role applying my data modeling and engineering skills to economic and policy challenges especially regarding the climate, cities, and regional development.
